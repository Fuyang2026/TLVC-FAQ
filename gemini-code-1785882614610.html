Before submitting a referral, please confirm that the student has been assigned/seen by a Teacher of the Visually Impaired (TVI).

A TVI is required for participation in a TLVC and must attend the clinic with the student. If the student is already receiving support from a TVI and the TVI believes that participation in a TLVC would benefit the student's educational programming, please follow the referral process and submit the required forms.

If you are unsure whether the student has a TVI, or your school authority is not currently connected to one, please contact the LISS Unit at ecc.liss@gov.ab.ca. We can work with the school authority to determine what supports are currently in place and help connect the student with appropriate services.

Clinic appointments are generally offered on a first-come, first-served basis once all required forms have been received. If all available clinic appointments have been filled, students may be placed on a waiting list or offered a spot at a nearby clinic location when appropriate.

For an overview of the referral process, please see here.

Once a referral is received, the LISS Unit reviews the information for completeness. To be considered for scheduling, the required referral documentation must be submitted, including the referral form and family questionnaire.

When clinic schedules are being finalized, the LISS Unit will typically contact the student's TVI approximately 3-4 weeks before the clinic to confirm availability and review scheduling considerations. Once the schedule is confirmed, the family and school team will be notified of the appointment details.

If you have questions about your referral or clinic availability, please contact ecc.liss@gov.ab.ca.

Students are encouraged bring any vision-related devices, technology, low vision aids, or equipment they currently use. Families and school teams are also encouraged to bring questions and examples of situations where the student experiences challenges at school, at home, or in the community. This information helps the clinic team develop practical and individualized recommendations. Recommendations regarding current and future use of assistive technology are part of the clinic process.

For questions about Travelling Low Vision Clinics, please contact the Low Incidence Supports and Services (LISS) Unit at ecc.liss@gov.ab.ca.

School team participation is strongly encouraged. Members of the student's learning team provide valuable information about the student's educational needs and classroom experiences and support follow-up implementation of recommendations after the appointment.
